Where to start? ¶

You can play Kingdom Hearts in any order — no way is more correct than another. Ours is just one take; feel free to modify it and make it your own.

New players ¶

If you've never played a Kingdom Hearts game, start with Kingdom Hearts (2002) and follow the series in narrative order from there.
