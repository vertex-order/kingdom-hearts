## Editions (ISBN)

- One label per book (any print edition): ISBN, else ASIN. Bullets under a label are that book's notes and sources.
- Three editions with different content, matching the three in `site/data/`:
  - 2003–2005 (Enterbrain JP, TokyoPop EN, foreign translations): base story + the original bonus chapters
  - 2006–2007 Final Mix (Gangan Comics JP, Yen Press EN 2013 in 2 books): base story + the Final Mix bonus chapters
  - 2025 (Panini EN): base story + the original bonus chapters + the Final Mix bonus chapters
- Each edition's labels match its own `subtitleDate` in the entry, so they are greppable.
- Yen Press's two "Final Mix" books are omnibuses (original volumes 1-2 and 3-4, episodes 1-25 and 26-42), so their numbers do not map to the 3 Japanese Final Mix volumes or to the 4 single TokyoPop volumes. Labelled "Yen Press omnibus N of 2" below.
- Unlike most entries, links here are not mixed across publishers: the three editions differ in content, so each page is for the edition being described.
- Goodreads stats (4.06, 4,860 ratings, 283 reviews) are Vol. 1's own work-wide figure, shared by its language printings. Each volume is a separate Goodreads work with its own rating.

## Decisions

- Three editions, not one: the 2006–2007 Final Mix is a revised edition with its own bonus chapters, not a rebind of the 2003–2005 original, and the 2025 Panini edition is the only one with both sets of bonus content.
  - `primary` is the 2025 edition; the other two are `primary.versions[]`, each differentiated by `subtitle: 'Edition'` and its own `subtitleDate`.
- Final Mix `subtitleDate` tracks the Japanese release (2006–2007), like the other two editions, not the 2013 Yen Press translation.
- Final Mix `languages` (JA) links to Google Books: none of its 5 ISBNs (3 JP, 2 Yen Press EN) are on Goodreads.
