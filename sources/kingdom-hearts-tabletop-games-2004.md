## wikipedia.org (Information)

- https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Kingdom_Hearts_media -- accessed 2026-10-24
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Kingdom_Hearts_media#Collectible_card_game -- dates*, story, publishers*
    - "first released in Japan to coincide with the release of Chain of Memories", produced by Tomy (reference: Game Informer, TGS 2004 Square Enix press conference wrap-up, 2004-09-24)
    - Contents: starter decks, playing mats and booster packs, sold in a variety of booster packs
    - Play: the player takes the role of Sora, travels to worlds with the cards and battles the Heartless; two players, who can play together with a deck and a "Player Card"
    - Fantasy Flight Games acquired the translation and North American marketing rights in 2007; released in North America 2007-11-15
