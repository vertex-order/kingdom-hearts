## Decisions

- `languages[]` (JA, EN, DE, ES, FR, FI, IT, PT) each link to a goodreads edition page -- swapped in over the earlier Wikipedia/Google Books placeholders now that goodreads covers all of them (per the goodreads > google books > open library priority). `ratings` uses the EN edition's goodreads page URL, scored with the 4.35 shown on the work (both JA-edition goodreads links and the EN link surface the same aggregate score).
- Italian has 3 distinct ISBN'd/ASIN'd Panini printings (all recorded in Editions above) -- `languages[IT]` links the newest, most complete one (ISBN 9788828771838, Nov 30 2023, Panini Comics - Planet Manga); the other two are a same-date/publisher ISBN'd variant and an earlier ASIN-only 2020 printing.
- Portuguese only exists as 3 Panini omnibus hardcovers (collecting Vol. 1-2, 2-4, 4-5) -- `languages[PT]` links the first one (Omnibus Vol. 1, ISBN 9786555123777) per the collection-over-single-volume preference.
- French also has a 2-volume Nobi Nobi "l'intégrale" omnibus (T03 collects Vol. 1-3, T04 collects Vol. 3-5) alongside the single-volume Vol. 1 printing already linked from `languages[FR]` -- recorded in Editions, not swapped in (single-volume Vol. 1 stays the representative link, consistent with every other language here).
- Several German ISBNs (Vol. 3 9783770477517, Vol. 4 9783770480234) show a different date/page-count on goodreads than what google books recorded earlier -- both source lines kept as found, not reconciled.
